# Remote Non-Clinical Healthcare Business/Tech Careers for a Texas PTA (PTA license + associate's + B.S. Kinesiology), 2026

Research date: 2026-10-06. Search budget was limited (~20 tool calls) and several sites (cco.us, remotive.com) were blocked by the network proxy, so some pathways are only partly sourced. Salary figures from aggregators (Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, practicetestgeeks.com) are self-reported or estimated. Treat them as rough, and prefer live postings.

## 1. Rehab therapy software companies (implementation, clinical success, support, trainer, sales)

### Takeaway
Rehab EMR and home-health/post-acute software vendors are the best fit for a PTA's background. Clinical implementation and consultant roles there use clinical workflow knowledge, are often remote, and pay roughly $70K–$110K. One correction to the premise: WebPT has **not** been folded into a larger company in 2025–26. It acquired Clinicient back in 2019. In January 2026 it **sold** its Keet Health outcomes product to Net Health.

### Cited Findings
- **WebPT status (verified):** In January 2026 Net Health acquired Keet Health, a WebPT company, as part of a "strategic collaboration" with WebPT. Net Health's Limber platform becomes WebPT's preferred outcomes/RTM (remote therapeutic monitoring) solution, with an integrated experience targeted for early 2026 — [Net Health blog, Jan 13 2026](https://www.nethealth.com/blog/net-health-advances-rehab-therapy-care-with-acquisition-of-keet-health-from-webpt/); [WebPT press page](https://www.webpt.com/about/press/net-health-advances-rehab-therapy-care-with-acquisition-of-keet-health-from-webpt); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/net-health-advances-rehab-therapy-care-with-acquisition-of-keet-health-from-webpt-302660013.html)
- WebPT acquired competitor Clinicient (the Insight EMR) earlier, which made it the market leader — [Arizona Technology Council](https://www.aztechcouncil.org/webpt-acquires-competitor-clinicient-cementing-valley-startups-role-as-market-leader/). Search summaries said Warburg Pincus has owned WebPT since 2019. This is not verified against a primary source; see [Wikipedia: WebPT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WebPT).
- Net Health also acquired Limber Health (the RTM/HEP platform, founded by PTs) — [PrivSource](https://www.privsource.com/acquisitions/deal/LXSgwq)
- **Raintree Systems:** EHR + revenue cycle management (RCM) for PT/OT/SLP. Described as remote-first. Has posted remote "Clinical Implementation Consultant" and "MIPS/Regulatory clinical implementation consultant" roles. Glassdoor shows Implementation Consultant pay of about **$72K–$109K** — [Raintree careers](https://www.raintreeinc.com/careers/); [Glassdoor Raintree](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Raintree-California-Salaries-EI_IE27251.0,8_IL.9,19_IS2280.htm); [wfh.team Raintree profile](https://wfh.team/companies/raintree-systems)
- **WellSky Clinical Consultant pay:** average about $113,873 on Glassdoor, with **$82K–$100K at 1–3 years' experience**. Indeed shows a higher estimate (about $134K average, $101K–$179K for the 25th–75th percentile). These are small samples (4 salaries on Glassdoor) and conflict with each other — [Glassdoor WellSky](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/WellSky-Clinical-Consultant-Salaries-E4942_D_KO8,27.htm); [Indeed WellSky](https://www.indeed.com/cmp/Wellsky/salaries/Clinical-Consultant)
- **HCHB (Homecare Homebase), headquartered in Dallas:** about 730 employees. Its "Senior Implementation Consultant, Clinical" role guides home health and hospice agencies through Discovery/Design/Build/Test and deployment, including remote deployment. It requires a bachelor's degree, **3+ years in home health or hospice**, and experience training on or supporting software. Its job mix is about 46% remote and 50% on-site — [BuiltIn Austin HCHB](https://www.builtinaustin.com/company/homecare-homebase/jobs); [Hired posting](https://www.hired.com/job/sr-implementation-consultant-hearst-merged-accounts)

### Inferences
- The HCHB posting suggests the "clinical" implementation track values hands-on clinical experience in the target setting. A PTA with home health or SNF experience fits HCHB, WellSky, PointClickCare, MatrixCare, and Net Health (which owns Casamba for SNF/home health therapy). A PTA with outpatient experience fits WebPT, Raintree, Prompt, SPRY, and MedBridge.
- The usual entry point is customer support or onboarding specialist (roughly $50–65K, not verified here). From there people move to implementation consultant, then clinical success or product roles. A PTA who has been the clinic's "EMR super-user" should say so on the resume.
- Search keywords: "clinical implementation consultant," "implementation specialist therapy," "clinical success manager," "clinical product specialist," "clinical trainer EMR," "rehab subject matter expert," "PT/PTA/OT/COTA preferred," "licensed clinician preferred," "therapy solutions consultant."

## 2. EHR clinical applications analyst / Epic and Oracle Health (Cerner)

### Takeaway
Epic certification can't be bought independently. It needs an employer (a health system, or a consulting firm working on an Epic project) to sponsor it. The realistic path for a PTA is to join a Texas health system that uses Epic, as a therapist or rehab super-user, and transfer internally into an analyst role.

### Cited Findings
- You cannot apply for Epic certification on your own. A sponsoring employer pays most of the costs. The common route is working at a hospital during an Epic implementation and volunteering or being nominated — [HealthcareITLeaders](https://www.healthcareitleaders.com/blog/how-to-become-epic-certified/)
- Epic Rehab analyst postings commonly require **internal** candidates to earn the module certification within about 120 days, while **external** hires must already be certified — [PIH Health Epic Application Analyst (Rehab & LTC)](https://jobs.pihhealth.org/job/whittier/epic-application-analyst-i-ii-iii-rehab-and-ltc/912/81999927696)
- A Texas example: UT Southwestern (Dallas) posted a "Senior EPIC Rehab Analyst" — [UTSW posting](https://jobs.utsouthwestern.edu/job/23369559/senior-epic-rehab-analyst/)

### Inferences
- Concrete path: get a PTA job, or a rehab tech/scheduling role, at an Epic-based Texas system such as UTSW, Baylor Scott & White, Texas Health Resources, Houston Methodist, or Memorial Hermann (Epic use is from general knowledge, not verified here). Then volunteer as a credentialed trainer or super-user during upgrades and apply internally for Epic Rehab or Ambulatory analyst roles. Analyst roles are often hybrid or remote after onboarding.
- Search keywords: "Epic Rehab analyst," "Epic credentialed trainer," "Epic principal trainer," "clinical applications analyst therapy," "Oracle Health/Cerner PowerChart therapy analyst."

### Gaps
- I did not get a cited 2026 salary range for Epic analysts or credentialed trainers.
- I did not get a Cerner/Oracle Health certification pathway.

## 3. Medical coding and billing (rehab-focused)

### Takeaway
A CPC is cheap and quick to earn, but the entry-level market is genuinely hard. CPC-A holders report many jobs ask for 1–2 years of experience. For a PTA, the better route is usually PT billing, RCM, or charge-review roles, where knowing the 97xxx codes, the 8-minute rule, KX modifiers, and plan-of-care documentation gives a real edge.

### Cited Findings
- **CPC exam cost:** about $399 for AAPC members and $499 for non-members, including one free retake. There are also voucher options. This comes from secondary prep sites, so verify on aapc.com — [practicetestgeeks CPC guide](https://practicetestgeeks.com/cpc/cpc-certification)
- **Removing the CPC-A "apprentice" designation:** two years of verified coding experience, OR one year of experience plus an 80-hour coding course or AAPC Practicode (600 cases at 70% or better) — [practicetestgeeks AAPC](https://practicetestgeeks.com/aapc/aapc-certifications); [AAPC forum thread "CPC-A removal"](https://aapc.com/discuss/threads/cpc-a-removal.194756). A CCO blog post titled "Elimination of AAPC Apprentice Credential" suggests AAPC may have changed or eliminated the apprentice credential. I couldn't read it (site blocked), so this is **unverified** — [CCO blog](https://www.cco.us/blog/elimination-of-aapc-apprentice-credential/)
- **Pay:** a secondary summary of the 2025 AAPC Salary Survey gives about $64,495 average for credentialed coders and about $62,500 for CPCs. New CPC-As start around $42K–$48K. Secondary source, so verify against AAPC — [practicetestgeeks salary outlook](https://practicetestgeeks.com/cpc/salary-outlook)
- **Remote share:** one secondary source says about 78% of CPC roles are remote or hybrid, while another says 55%+ work remotely at least part-time. The figures conflict and are low quality — [practicetestgeeks CPC jobs](https://practicetestgeeks.com/cpc/certified-professional-coder-jobs)
- **Market competitiveness:** AAPC forum posts from 2025–26 CPC-A holders say most postings require 1–2 years' experience. One person who certified in Nov 2025 got in through a Revenue Integrity internship and advised "get your foot in the door in revenue cycle in general." Others report applying to 50+ jobs — [AAPC forum: CPC-A looking for entry-level](https://www.aapc.com/discuss/threads/cpc-a-looking-for-entry-level-positions.205869/); [AAPC forum: CPC-A seeking 100% remote](https://www.aapc.com/discuss/threads/cpc-a-seeking-100-remote-entry-level-medical-coding-position-%E2%80%93-iowa.236548/)

### Inferences
- An AHIMA CCS is aimed at inpatient facility coding and is less relevant to outpatient rehab. A CPC (outpatient/professional) fits better. No widely recognized "rehab-only" coding credential turned up.
- A CPC is a modest resume booster for rehab RCM, compliance, or EMR billing-implementation roles. On its own it is a weak standalone entry ticket in 2026.

### Gaps
- I didn't verify current CPC exam pricing or AAPC membership cost on aapc.com.
- I didn't confirm whether AAPC changed the CPC-A designation in 2025–26.
- AHIMA CCS cost and eligibility were not fetched.

## 4. Rehab revenue cycle management and PT billing companies

### Takeaway
Remote PT billing jobs are plentiful but low-paid at entry, around $18–20/hr. Most ask for prior PT billing experience. A PTA's documentation and coding knowledge helps, but these are not premium jobs. The better-paid targets are RCM roles at EMR vendors (for example Raintree RCM) or denial and compliance specialist roles.

### Cited Findings
- **Bodies in Balance PT:** fully remote medical biller/coder at **$18–20/hr**, 3 years' billing experience preferred — [CareerPlug posting](https://bodies-in-balance-physical-therapy.careerplug.com/jobs/2114742)
- Remote PT billing postings found: HENO ($18–20/hr); FYZICAL (full-cycle biller, 12–24+ months' experience); a billing resolution specialist role for imaging and PT (3+ years' PT RCM); and Argo IQ Billing (1099 contractor, 10–15 hrs/week, 3+ years' outpatient PT billing). These come from search-result summaries of job boards and were not individually fetched — [Coding Ahead PT biller](https://www.codingahead.com/jobs/a1ea89f9-96ec-49c2-8892-eeb1ae284875/physical-therapy-medical-biller); [Coding Ahead full revenue cycle PT](https://webhooks.codingahead.com/jobs/a1ea8989-8eae-4eb4-b581-dfeeb402ff52/remote-medical-biller-full-revenue-cycle-physical-therapy); [Virtual Vocations](https://www.virtualvocations.com/job/billing-resolution-specialist-3137638-i.html)
- Raintree sells RCM services alongside its EHR — [Raintree careers](https://www.raintreeinc.com/careers/)

### Inferences
- A PTA would likely compete mainly on clinical documentation knowledge: medical necessity, plan-of-care certification, functional limitation reporting, MIPS. Roles such as "documentation compliance auditor," "therapy compliance specialist," or "denials specialist – therapy" fit better than data-entry billing.
- Search keywords: "physical therapy biller remote," "therapy revenue cycle," "rehab billing specialist," "therapy compliance auditor," "MDS/therapy reimbursement."

### Gaps

- I found no posting that explicitly prefers former PTAs.

## 5. Clinical Documentation Integrity (CDI)

### Takeaway
CDI is technically open to a PTA but realistically hard to enter. The CCDS exam requires three years of **inpatient acute** CDI experience and current employment as a CDI specialist, and hiring is RN- and coder-dominated.

### Cited Findings
- **ACDIS CCDS eligibility:** candidates with an associate's degree (or equivalent) in an allied health field qualify with **3 years** as a concurrent or retrospective CDS in an inpatient acute facility using IPPS, plus college coursework in medical terminology and A&P. An alternate route uses an AHIMA CCS/CCS-P plus 3 years' CDS experience. Candidates must currently work as a CDI specialist — [ACDIS update on CCDS requirements](https://www.acdis.org/articles/acdis-update-requirements-and-experience-necessary-test-ccds-credential); [ACDIS CCDS handbook](https://acdis.org/sites/acdis/files/Handbook_CCDS_CCDS-O_Final_0.pdf)

### Inferences
- There is a chicken-and-egg problem: you need a CDI job to get the CCDS. Outpatient CDI and the ACDIS CCDS-O, or a CCS plus coding experience, could be side doors. A PTA is unlikely to be hired into inpatient CDI ahead of RNs without coding experience.

### Gaps
- I did not check AHIMA CDIP eligibility.
- I did not get a cited CDI salary.

## 6. Medical writing, CE content, exam prep, and teaching in PTA programs

### Takeaway
Content and education roles exist (MedBridge clinical content, CE companies, exam prep), but full-time ones are few and often go to PTs or DPTs. Teaching in a PTA program is a credible path for a PTA with a bachelor's degree, though CAPTE programs have lab components, so most of that teaching is in person.

### Cited Findings
- MedBridge hires remote staff in Texas and has clinical content roles: a Senior Clinical Content Program Manager producing patient exercise and education programs, and content writers/training developers — [MedBridge posting](https://recruiting.paylocity.com/Recruiting/Jobs/Details/2779226). MedBridge course instructors are a separate, contract-style "medical experts" track — [MedBridge instructors](https://medbridge.com/instructors)
- **PTA program adjunct requirements (example postings):** a current, unrestricted PT or PTA license, about **4 years of clinical experience**, and a **bachelor's degree for PTA applicants**. A master's is preferred. Credentials should be at least equivalent to the credential the program awards and consistent with CAPTE/ACCSC expectations. The duties are in-person lecture and lab teaching — [Stanbridge PTA adjunct](https://apply.workable.com/stanbridge/jobs/view/E886D63781.md); [AcademicJobs PTA adjunct](https://academicjobs.com/university-jobs/adjunct-faculty-physical-therapist-assistant-pta-program/699198)
- **Texas/SACSCOC rules:** faculty teaching non-transfer associate (AAS) technical courses need a bachelor's in the teaching discipline, or an associate's degree plus demonstrated competencies — [TAMU-Commerce credential guidelines](https://inside.tamuc.edu/facultystaffservices/humanResources/documents/Guidelines%20for%20Validating%20Instructor%20Credentials%20to%20Teach.pdf). Lamar State College Orange (Texas) also accepts an industry credential plus 3 years of full-time work experience for workforce AAS courses — [LSCO faculty credential guidelines](https://lsco.edu/about/accreditation/_documents/lsco-faculty-credentials-guidelines-updated-april-2024.pdf)

### Inferences
- With a PTA license, a B.S. in kinesiology, and several years of practice, this person likely meets adjunct requirements at many Texas community college PTA programs. Adjunct pay is low and per course. "Online" PTA teaching is mostly the didactic part of hybrid programs, and CAPTE programs require in-person labs. Becoming a clinical instructor for students and earning the APTA CCIP credential first strengthens the application. Full-time core faculty or the ACCE role usually require more, often a master's.
- Freelance health writing and exam-prep item writing (Scorebuilders, TherapyEd, PESI/Summit/Relias CE) are realistic side income rather than an immediate full-time replacement.
- Search keywords: "PTA program adjunct Texas," "clinical content specialist," "exercise content," "patient education writer," "item writer PTA exam," "instructional designer healthcare."

### Gaps
- I didn't verify the CAPTE PTA standard on core faculty degree requirements from capteonline.org.
- I got no 2026 salary data for MedBridge content roles, adjunct pay at Texas colleges, or freelance health writing rates.
- I didn't check Scorebuilders, TherapyEd, PESI, Summit, Relias, or Gaylord hiring.

## 7. Medical device / DME / orthotics (inside sales and clinical specialist)

### Takeaway
Device and DME sales can pay well but is mostly field-based, territory-driven, and commission-heavy. One cautionary example: **Zynex (TENS) filed Chapter 11 in December 2025** in the Southern District of Texas and has cut staff heavily.

### Cited Findings
- **Zynex:** filed Chapter 11 on Dec 15 2025 (S.D. Tex., Case No. 25-90810). It was delisted from Nasdaq and now trades as ZYXIQ. A Third Amended Plan of Reorganization was filed Feb 7 2026 — [SEC 8-K, Dec 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/846475/000110465925121390/zyxi-20251215x8k.htm); [SEC 8-K, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/846475/000110465926032723/zyxi-20260319x8k.htm). Third-party data shows about 543 employees in 2026, down about 12.7% from 2025 — [Revelio Labs](https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/zynex/employees)

### Gaps
- I didn't research Breg, Enovis/DJO, Hanger, or Bioness remote clinical specialist roles, or their pay. Those companies' clinical specialist roles are generally field-based, not remote (from general knowledge, not verified).

## 8. Remote clinical recruiter at therapy staffing firms

### Takeaway
Recruiting is one of the most accessible fully remote pivots. Aya's remote Travel Therapy Recruiter role has advertised **$60K base plus commission**. A PTA background helps with credibility with candidates, but postings ask for metrics-driven sales or staffing experience.

### Cited Findings
- **Aya Healthcare, Travel Therapy Recruiter:** remote, full-time, **$60,000 starting base plus commission**, recruiting PT/OT/SLP for travel jobs. Requires a bachelor's degree and 1+ years (2+ preferred) in metrics-driven sales or staffing. Benefits include free premium medical, dental, and vision and a 401(k) match. This is from a search summary of a remote job board; the page itself was blocked — [Remotive Aya listing](https://remotive.com/remote/jobs/human-resources/travel-therapy-recruiter-5283200); [Remoteleaf Aya listing](https://remoteleaf.com/company/aya-healthcare/travel-therapy-recruiter-united-states-2/)

### Inferences
- The kinesiology bachelor's satisfies the degree requirement. The PTA should frame patient scheduling and caseload work, plus any sales or customer-facing experience, as metrics-driven. Texas-based therapy staffing (for example Medical Solutions or Fusion offices, and Dallas/Houston home-health staffing) wasn't verified here.
- Search keywords: "travel therapy recruiter remote," "allied recruiter therapy," "rehab recruiter," "clinical liaison therapy staffing."

### Gaps
- I didn't verify recruiter pay or remote status at AMN, Cross Country, Medical Solutions, Jackson Therapy Partners, Fusion, or Advantage Rehab, or whether they explicitly prefer former clinicians.
