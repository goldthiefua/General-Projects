# Remote Utilization Review, Utilization Management, Prior Authorization, Case Management and Payer Roles for a Texas PTA (AAS) with a Kinesiology BS (2026)

Method note: research ran 2026-10-06. Every WebFetch attempt (ccmcertification.org, tdi.texas.gov, ziprecruiter.com, alternativehealthcarecareers.com, thenonclinicalpt.com, remotenursingjobs.com) was blocked by the network egress proxy. All findings below therefore come from search-engine result snippets and summaries of the cited pages, not from full page reads. ## Q1. Which remote UR/UM/prior-auth/clinical-reviewer roles accept PTA or COTA licenses, and which require PT/OT/RN?

### Takeaway
Most named payer and benefit-manager therapy "clinical reviewer" roles (eviCore/Evernorth, Evolent/NIA, Optum, BCBS "PT UM reviewer") require a full PT (or OT/SLP) license and often many years of experience, so a PTA cannot get them. PTA- or COTA-eligible reviewer roles do exist, but they are exceptions. Career blogs cite Anthem (Elevance/Carelon), myNEXUS (home-health UM, now part of Elevance) and Numotion as employers that have hired assistants. Some nurse-level (LPN/LVN) UR roles also list allied licenses. Confirm each one by reading the exact license list in the posting.

### Cited Findings
- **eviCore (Cigna/Evernorth), "Clinical Reviewer – Physical Therapist" (remote, posted from Bloomfield, CT):** reported minimum of **ten years of clinical experience** and a **valid Physical Therapy license in state of residence**. This is PT only, not PTA. — [Career.com listing of Cigna/eviCore posting (2023)](https://www.career.com/job/cigna/clinical-reviewer-physical-therapist-evicore-remote/j202305301111122315345). The posting is dated 2023; the 2026 version was not verified.
- eviCore runs PT/OT utilization management for health plans. For example, a May 2026 provider presentation covers "WellCare PT/OT Management", which means real reviewer demand exists. Reviews route to specialty reviewers by discipline (PT, chiropractor, acupuncturist, massage therapist). — [eviCore 2026 WellCare PT/OT presentation](https://www.evicore.com/sites/default/files/resources/2026-05/2026%20WellCare%20PTOT%20Management_%20Provider%20Presentation%2020260526.pdf); [eviCore Physical Medicine FAQ](https://beonbrand.getbynder.com/m/2c6424f0b03161ea/original/Physical-Medicine-FAQ.pdf)
- **Evolent (formerly NIA), "Clinical Reviewer, Physical Therapy" (remote, UM team):** requires **current, unrestricted state licensure as a Physical Therapist**. A PTA license does not qualify. — [Glassdoor listing](https://www.glassdoor.com/job-listing/clinical-reviewer-physical-therapy-evolent-JV_KO0,34_KE35,42.htm?jl=1009835328393)
- **Optum, "Clinical Review – Physical Therapy":** ZipRecruiter aggregates these at **$87k–$106k**. These are PT roles. — [ZipRecruiter Optum Clinical Review PT](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Optum-Clinical-Review-Physical-Therapy)
- **American Specialty Health (ASH):** has posted a "CQE Manager I – Rehabilitation Services – Physical Therapy" remote role. The search summary did not confirm the license requirement; ASH clinical quality evaluator roles are generally PT-level. — [search summary citing ZipRecruiter/Indeed aggregations](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Remote-Utilization-Review-Physical-Therapist)
- **BCBS of Massachusetts, "PT Utilization Management Reviewer":** this is a PT-titled role. — [Jooble listing](https://jooble.org/jdp/-1544404299087636758)
- A career site for rehab professionals states that "COTAs and PTAs can apply for utilization reviewer positions." It also says most cases require a PT, OT or SLP license, but "some companies make exceptions for assistants." It names **Anthem, MyNexus, and Numotion** as companies that hire assistants as utilization reviewers. These roles typically require an active unrestricted license and several years of clinical experience, and are usually full-time, 100% remote, roughly 9am–5pm. — [Alternative Healthcare Careers: Remote jobs for PT and OT assistants](https://www.alternativehealthcarecareers.com/blog/remote-jobs-for-pt-and-ot-assistants); [AHC: Utilization Reviewer](https://www.alternativehealthcarecareers.com/blog/utilization-review). The underlying postings and their dates were not viewable.
- A remote "Utilization Review Health Care Professional (PT, LVN, LPN)" posting lists acceptable credentials as **RN, DC, PT, DPT, LVN, OT, LAc**. **PTA is not listed.** Other reported details: 2 years of clinical experience; 1 year of UM experience required; non-exempt full-time role, Mon–Fri, 8:30am–6pm Pacific; candidates can live in any state except CA, NY, HI, DC (so Texas is eligible). The employer was not visible in the snippet. — [RemoteNursingJobs posting](https://remotenursingjobs.com/job/utilization-review-health-care-professional-pt-lvn-lpn-remote/)
- **Elevance/Carelon UM roles:** clinical UM roles ask for RN or **LPN/LVN** licensure. The non-licensed "Utilization Management Representative I" does precert and prior-auth intake: eligibility and benefit checks, entering referral requests into the UM system, and phone inquiries. It is virtual except for required in-person training. — [Lensa: Elevance UM Rep I](https://lensa.com/job-v1/elevance-health/remote/utilization-management-nurse/00150c0eaea0d500d7d0e5fbaa8c4b43); [ZipRecruiter Carelon UR](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Elevance-Health-Carelon-Utilization-Review)
- Indeed returned about 700 results for "utilization review PTA". This is a keyword count, not 700 PTA-eligible jobs, and most are likely PT/RN roles. — [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/q-utilization-review-pta-jobs-jobs.html)

### Inferences
- For a PTA, the realistic payer-side tiers are:
  - **(a) Non-licensed UM/prior-auth representative or intake roles** (Elevance UM Rep, health-plan authorization coordinators, therapy-authorization specialists at outpatient clinic groups). These are the most accessible.
  - **(b) The occasional assistant-eligible therapy UR role** (historically Anthem/myNEXUS home-health therapy UR and Numotion seating/mobility authorization). These are competitive and sporadic.
  - **(c) PT-licensed clinical reviewer roles** at eviCore, Evolent, Optum, ASH and BCBS. A PTA realistically cannot get these.
- Medical-necessity determinations are generally reserved for licensed professionals who can practice independently. That likely explains why plans prefer PT/RN. The PTA's supervised scope is a structural barrier, not just employer preference. This is reasoning, not a sourced statement.
- No source confirmed PTA eligibility at Optum, Carelon/AIM, Tivity/WholeHealth Networks, Helix, OrthoNet, Palladian, MRIoA, MCMC, Dane Street, ExamWorks, Humana, Superior HealthPlan, Molina, BCBSTX/HCSC, Texas Children's Health Plan, UnitedHealthcare or Zelis. Peer-review and IRO firms (MRIoA, MCMC, Dane Street, ExamWorks) contract physicians and full-scope licensed therapists, so a PTA is very unlikely to qualify there (inference).

### Gaps
- No full 2026 posting was readable because pages were blocked. I could not confirm any 2026 posting that literally lists "PTA" as a qualifying license. I also could not verify myNEXUS/Anthem PTA eligibility directly, or what Numotion's authorization roles require.
- No data was found on ASH, Tivity/WholeHealth, Helix, OrthoNet, Palladian, Humana, Molina, Superior, BCBSTX, TCHP, Cigna or Zelis license rules for therapy reviewers.

## Q2. Non-licensed intake, prior-auth coordinator, authorization specialist and care coordinator roles

### Takeaway
These are the most realistic entry points for a PTA. They are remote-friendly and clinical knowledge is a plus, but the pay is clerical-level: about $16–$24/hr in Texas, with specialty pharmacy and infusion roles reaching $23–$27/hr.

- ERI SalaryExpert puts the Texas prior-auth specialist average at **$47,889/yr (about $23/hr)**: entry level (1–3 years) about $36,003, senior (8+ years) about $53,363. — [SalaryExpert](https://www.salaryexpert.com/salary/job/specialist-prior-authorization/united-states/texas)

## Q3. Workers' compensation roles and the Texas adjuster license

### Takeaway
Workers' comp claims adjusting is the strongest non-RN, payer-side path for a Texas PTA with a bachelor's degree. It needs a Texas all-lines adjuster license, which costs about $50 plus fingerprinting and a course or exam, and pays about $60k–$81k. WC field and telephonic case manager roles at Sedgwick, CorVel and Genex mostly need an RN or a credential such as CCM/CDMS/CRC.

### Cited Findings
- **Texas all-lines adjuster license:**
  - Qualify by passing the Pearson VUE exam (150 questions, 70% to pass, 180 minutes) or by completing a TDI-approved 40-hour pre-licensing course, which waives the exam.
  - Apply to TDI through Sircon within 12 months; the application fee is **$50**, and a $25 late fee applies after one year.
  - Most applicants need IdentoGO fingerprinting.
  - The license covers residential, commercial, auto, farm and ranch, inland and ocean marine, and **workers' compensation** claims.
  - Sources: [TDI: Adjuster all lines](https://www.tdi.texas.gov/agent/adjuster-all-lines-apply.html); [Open Exam Prep TX adjuster (TIC 4101)](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/claims-adjuster/texas-statutes-rules/texas-adjuster-licensing); [Aceable](https://insurance.ecom-dev.aceable.com/resources/pre-license/texas-all-lines-adjuster/)
- **Texas Mutual Insurance (Austin) WC claims adjuster:** Glassdoor average **$68,025**. A separate claims adjuster average is **$65,518** (range $60K–$81K, 24 salaries reported). — [Glassdoor WC adjuster](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Texas-Mutual-Workers-Comp-Claims-Adjuster-Salaries-E19924_D_KO13,41.htm); [Glassdoor claims adjuster](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Texas-Mutual-Insurance-Company-Claims-Adjuster-Salaries-E19924_D_KO31,46.htm)
- Remote claims adjusters in Texas average **$60,193** (June 30, 2026). The ZipRecruiter range for remote Texas adjusters is $47k–$80k. Remote WC jobs in Texas pay $5,050–$6,909 per month. — [ZipRecruiter TX remote claims adjuster](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Remote-Claims-Adjuster/--in-Texas); [ZipRecruiter TX remote WC](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Remote-Workers-Compensation/--in-Texas)
- **Sedgwick Field Case Manager:** RN license **or** a graduate degree in health or human services **plus** CCM, CDMS or CRC. Needs a valid driver's license and high-speed internet. — [Workopia Sedgwick posting](https://workopia.io/jobs/4905afd60c49d0db789c005e113eb849)
- **Genex case manager:** a current, unrestricted license or certification in a health or human services discipline, plus pursuing URAC-recognized certification (CCM, CDMS, CRC, CRRN, COHN) once eligible. — [ZipRecruiter Genex](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Genex-Case-Management); [Indeed Genex reviews](https://www.indeed.com/cmp/Genex-Services,-LLC-1/reviews?fcountry=ALL&fjobtitle=Case+Manager)
- **CorVel case manager (one posting):** RN licensure in the state, 3+ years of clinical experience (rehab preferred), CCM preferred. — [Indeed CorVel case manager](https://www.indeed.com/q-corvel-case-manager-jobs.html)

### Inferences
- Genex's wording, "license or certification to practice a health or human services discipline", might technically let a PTA through, but the PTA's supervised scope makes that uncertain. Confirm with the recruiter. A PTA's real advantage in WC claims is reading therapy notes, judging the medical necessity of PT visits, and understanding functional capacity evaluations (FCEs) and return-to-work plans. That background helps in adjuster interviews.
- A realistic Texas path: get the all-lines (or WC-focused) license, apply for trainee or associate WC adjuster roles at Texas Mutual, Sedgwick, CorVel, Gallagher Bassett or carriers, and pursue CDMS after 2080 hours.
- One Call, Paradigm and Coventry (now Enlyte) were not verified.

### Gaps
- Pearson VUE exam and course costs were not confirmed (a $50 fee covers the license only). No sourced data on whether Texas Mutual hires adjusters fully remote rather than hybrid in Austin, or on Texas WC therapy UR roles under TDI-DWC URA rules.

## Q4. Case management and UR certifications: is a PTA eligible?

### Takeaway
ACM (ACMA): **no**, it is RN or social worker only. CCM (CCMC): uncertain to unlikely through the license route, because the license must allow **independent** practice. There is a possible route through a bachelor's degree in a health or human services field, but it requires a degree with **supervised case-management field experience** in the curriculum plus 24 months of case management work. CDMS: **yes, realistically**, because it accepts a bachelor's in any discipline plus 2080 hours of qualifying paid work. CPUR and CPHM (McKesson) are discontinued.

### Cited Findings
- **ACM (ACMA):** open only to RNs and social workers who practise case management in a health delivery system. Physical therapists are not an eligible discipline. — [ACMA FAQ](https://acmaweb.org/acm/default.aspx?nav=faq); [Open Exam Prep ACM eligibility](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/acma-acm/introduction/eligibility-fees-recertification)
- **CCM license route:** a current license or certification in a health or human services discipline that allows the holder **to practice independently**, plus 12 months of full-time case management employment in the past 5 years (or 12 months supervising CCMs). PTAs work under PT supervision and "may not meet the 'ability to practice independently' requirement". Verify with CCMC. — [Practice Test Geeks CCM requirements 2026](https://practicetestgeeks.com/ccm/ccm-certification-requirements). This is a secondary source; ccmcertification.org was blocked.
- **CCM degree route:** a bachelor's or graduate degree in social work, nursing, or another health or human services field that promotes physical, psychosocial or vocational well-being. The degree must be from an accredited school and **include supervised field experience in case management, health, or behavioral health**. Category 2 requires 24 months of full-time case management employment, with no CCM supervision needed. At least 20% of work time must be case management. — [CCMC blog "Know before you apply"](https://yourcommission.org/blog/know-you-apply-are-you-eligible-take-ccm-exam); [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/articles/case-management-certification); [Case Management Study Guide](https://casemanagementstudyguide.com/eligibility-for-the-certified-case-manager-exam-ccm-exam/)
- **CDMS:** a bachelor's degree in **any discipline** (CHEA-accredited) or an RN license, plus **2080 hours in the past 5 years** of paid work covering at least 2 of the 4 domains (disability, case management, workers' comp, wage replacement). Internships and volunteer work do not count. — [CDMS eligibility](https://cdms.org/get-certified/cdms-certification/eligibility-criteria-and-requirements); [CCMC CDMS eligibility at a glance](https://yourcommission.org/certification/certified-disability-management-specialist/get-certified/eligibility-at-a-glance)
- **CPUR and CPHM (McKesson/InterQual):** CPUR is no longer offered, and McKesson stopped offering CPHM certification and recertification as of December 2014. InterQual training is separate from certification. — [allnurses CPHM thread](https://allnurses.com/certified-professional-healthcare-management-cphm-t543335/); [allnurses CPUR/CPUM](https://allnurses.com/cpur-cpum-t100838/). These are forum sources, and the CPHM date is older data.
- Commercial "Utilization Management and MCG" courses exist for people who need familiarity with MCG criteria. — [Case Management Institute](https://casemanagementinstitute.com/utilization-management-and-mcg-course/)

### Inferences
- A kinesiology BS is a health-related degree, but it may not satisfy CCM's requirement for supervised case-management field experience in the curriculum, unless the program had a qualifying internship. CCMC would decide that case by case.
- CDMS is the most attainable recognized credential for this person. It is also one of the certs Sedgwick and Genex list. It needs about 1 year of qualifying paid work first, such as a WC adjuster, disability claims or UM intake role.
- Familiarity with InterQual and MCG appears as a "preferred" skill in UR postings. No single current public certification replaces CPUR.

### Gaps
- CCMC's 2026 primary page could not be read, so whether an assistant-level license qualifies under Category 1 is unconfirmed. No sourced data was found on ABQAURP HCQM eligibility for PTAs.

## Q5. Pay comparison (PTA vs PT/RN versions) and part-time/PRN options

### Takeaway
PT-licensed remote reviewers earn about $33–$59/hr and $87k–$106k at Optum. PTA-accessible intake and prior-auth roles earn about $16–$27/hr, and WC adjusters earn about $60k–$81k. Part-time or PRN remote UR exists mostly for physicians and full-scope clinicians. Assistant-level remote UR is mostly full-time, business hours.

### Cited Findings
- Remote PT UR pays $33–$59/hr; remote PT reviewers $1,596–$3,080 per week; remote OT reviewers $38–$115/hr. — [ZipRecruiter remote PT UR](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Remote-Physical-Therapy-Utilization-Review); [ZipRecruiter remote PT reviewer](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Remote-Physical-Therapy-Reviewer); [ZipRecruiter remote OT reviewer](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Remote-Occupational-Therapy-Reviewer)
- PT/OT UR jobs pay **$22–$50/hr** (ZipRecruiter aggregate). — [ZipRecruiter PT OT UR](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Pt-Ot-Utilization-Review)
- Optum PT clinical review pays $87k–$106k. — [ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Optum-Clinical-Review-Physical-Therapy)
- Remote LPN UR (the comparable non-RN licensed tier) pays $33–$59/hr per ZipRecruiter's aggregate. — [ZipRecruiter remote LPN UR](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Remote-Lpn-Utilization-Review)
- Assistant-eligible UR roles are described as typically full-time, 100% remote, 9–5. — [AHC](https://www.alternativehealthcarecareers.com/blog/remote-jobs-for-pt-and-ot-assistants)
- PRN remote UR postings exist, for example "PRN – Remote Utilization Review for Endocrinologist", but these are physician-level. — [Built In](https://builtin.com/job/prn-remote-utilization-review-endocrinologist/7339089)

### Inferences
- If a PTA lands an assistant-eligible therapy UR role, it would probably pay below PT reviewer rates, plausibly in the $25–$35/hr range. This is not sourced; treat it as an estimate.
- The prior-auth and intake tier (about $19–$23/hr) may be a pay cut from clinical PTA work in Texas.

### Gaps
- No PTA-specific UR salary data was found. Part-time remote options for non-licensed authorization roles were not quantified.

## Q6. Realistic entry paths, keywords and job boards

### Takeaway
The most realistic sequence is to (1) take a non-licensed UM, intake or prior-auth role, or a WC adjuster trainee role with the Texas license, (2) build 1–2 years of UM, WC or disability experience, and (3) add CDMS. In parallel, apply to the rare assistant-eligible therapy UR roles, especially home-health and DME/seating authorization.

### Cited Findings
- UR roles commonly want authorization or case management experience and 3–5 years of clinical experience, "though new graduates have been hired". — [AHC: Utilization Reviewer](https://www.alternativehealthcarecareers.com/blog/utilization-review); [ZipRecruiter career guide](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/career/PT-OT-Utilization-Review/What-Is-How-to-Become)
- Desired skills: EMR, UM software, and insurance guidelines and medical-necessity criteria. — [AHC](https://www.alternativehealthcarecareers.com/blog/utilization-review)
- Job boards that surfaced relevant listings: Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor, SimplyHired, Built In, Greenhouse company boards, Lensa, Jooble, RemoteNursingJobs and RemoteMedicalJobs (which list LPN/LVN UR roles; check for allied-license acceptance). — [RemoteMedicalJobs LPN UR](https://www.remotemedicaljobs.com/jobs/remote-licensed-utilization-review-ii-lpn-lvn-rn-in-nationwide/); [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/q-utilization-review-pta-jobs-jobs.html)
- Medicare covers PT services provided by a licensed PTA under PT supervision, as state law dictates. This underlines that a PTA's clinical authority is supervised. — [CMS LCD L33942 Home Health PT](https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/lcd.aspx?lcdId=33942&ver=48)
